    Since I was six years old, I have dreamed of studying medicine and saving lives.

    Over the years, my interests have grown to include statistical genetics, bioinformatics, software engineering, large language models, and AI for human health.

    Explore my journey↓

  2. 2013 to 2018

    Jilin, China

    I earned a Bachelor of Medicine in Preventive Medicine at Jilin University, with clinical rotations across several specialties. From 2016 to 2019 I was also a public health intern at the Jilin CDC, working on HIV/AIDS prevention and disease surveillance.

  3. 2019 to 2021

    St. Louis, United States

    I came to the United States for an MPH in Epidemiology and Biostatistics at the Brown School of Washington University in St. Louis.

  4. 2021 to 2026

    Minneapolis, United States

    I completed my Ph.D. in Epidemiology at the University of Minnesota, with minors in Biostatistics and Data Science. My research combined statistical genetics, proteomics, causal inference, and machine learning across six international cohorts. I served as a primary analyst on three NIH-funded research projects with approximately $6.6 million in combined funding.

    NIH grants: R01HL059367 · R01HL155209 · R21AG072530

  5. 2024, then 2025 to now

    Labcorp

    I joined Labcorp as an intern in 2024 and trained deep learning models to predict HIV drug resistance from genomic data. Since 2025 I have been a Bioinformatics Scientist there, building clinical genomics software and models on AWS.
